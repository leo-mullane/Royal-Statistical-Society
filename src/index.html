RSS prototype contents

Royal Statistical Society: website prototype

A clickable greyscale prototype of the whole new rss.org.uk, for agreeing structure, navigation and journeys before design. It covers the Must and Should requirements; Could requirements, including ClerksWell's suggestions, are flagged where relevant but not built.

Open the home page

How to review

  • Annotations are off. Switch them on in the dark bar at the top of every page. Yellow notes explain behaviour and CMS capability; red notes are questions for RSS. Each note names the requirement it answers, e.g. REQ-201.

  • View as (dark bar) switches between signed out, a registered non-member, a member and a lapsed member, so you can see what changes for each.

  • Navigation, search, filters, the calendar, tabs, accordions, forms (with validation), the basket and the cookie banner all work. Individual article and profile links go nowhere by design.

  • Search is fully working. Try "CStat", "bayseian" (a typo) or "census" (found inside a PDF).

  • All people, employers, fees and figures are placeholders.

Navigation

  • Eight task-based sections replace the nine organisational ones: Membership, Professional development, Events and training, Get involved, Policy and research, News and publications, Resources, About.

  • The four use-based hubs from RSS's IA document (Professional development, Events and training, Get involved with volunteering, Resources) are top-level sections, not pages under Membership.

  • A utility bar links the RSS family of sites (Significance, Real World Data Science, RSS Publications), MyRSS, the basket and Join.

  • Every mega-menu panel links to its section's landing page, and two panels have search built in (events and experts).

Module library. The requirements trace is in requirements/requirements.md in the repository.
