About the Royal Statistical Society

We are a professional body for statisticians and data scientists, and a charity that promotes statistics for the public good. We were founded in 1834 and received our royal charter in 1887.

Our strategy

Our strategy has four aims: to support statisticians and data scientists throughout their careers, to advance the discipline, to promote statistical literacy, and to influence policy.

Governance

The Society is governed by its Council, elected by the membership, and supported by committees and staff.
