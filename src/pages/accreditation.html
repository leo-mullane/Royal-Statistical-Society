Accreditation

We accredit university degrees and data science programmes that meet our standards, so that graduates can apply for GradStat or DSP status more easily.

Routes to accreditation

University accreditation

For undergraduate and master's degrees with substantial statistical content.

Alliance for Data Science Professionals (AfDSP)

For programmes that lead to Data Science Professional status.

How it works

Tell us about your programme using the form. If it looks eligible, we will invite you to apply in the accreditation portal, where you can track your application and see past ones.
