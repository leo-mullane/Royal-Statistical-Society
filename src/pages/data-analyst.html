Data Analyst

Data Analyst status recognises people who work with data professionally, whatever their route in, including apprenticeships.

CMSStandard content page: left-hand section menu, rich text with headings, and reusable components (key facts, steps, accordion, call to action) placed by editors. REQ-029, REQ-013, REQ-014

At a glance

Who it is for
Analysts with relevant experience, or who have completed a Level 4 data analyst apprenticeship
Fee
£[fee] application fee, then the Data Analyst annual fee
How long it takes
Usually 4 to 6 weeks
You will need
CV, a short statement of your work, and one referee or your apprenticeship certificate

How to apply

  1. Join as a Fellow
  2. Apply in MyRSS
  3. Assessment
  4. Award

You apply from MyRSS once you are a member. Your application is assessed by the Professional Affairs Committee, which may ask for more evidence.

Once awarded, you will appear on the public Professional Register unless you choose not to.
