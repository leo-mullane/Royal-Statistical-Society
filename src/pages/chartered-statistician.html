Chartered Statistician (CStat)

CStat is the Society's highest professional award. It shows employers and clients that you have the qualifications, experience and commitment to professional development of a senior statistician.

At a glance

Who it is for
Statisticians with an approved degree and at least five years of professional experience
Fee
£[fee] application fee, then the CStat annual fee
How long it takes
Usually 8 to 12 weeks from a complete application
You will need
Degree certificate, CV, two referees, CPD record

How to apply

  1. Join as a Fellow
  2. Apply in MyRSS
  3. Assessment
  4. Award

You apply from MyRSS once you are a member. Your application is assessed by the Professional Affairs Committee, which may ask for more evidence.

Once awarded, you will appear on the public Professional Register unless you choose not to.
