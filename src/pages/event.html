Discussion meeting: Statistical aspects of evaluating large language models

A paper read to the Society, followed by an open discussion. Contributions can be made in the room or online.

Large language models are increasingly evaluated on benchmark datasets, often with little attention to sampling, uncertainty or the dependence between test items. The paper proposes a statistical framework for evaluation and shows how conclusions change when uncertainty is taken into account.

The paper will be published in Series A with the discussion. Written contributions can be submitted for two weeks after the meeting.
