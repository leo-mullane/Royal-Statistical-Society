The Society welcomes the report's focus on user needs and sets out three priorities for implementation.

The Royal Statistical Society has responded to the Statistics Commission's final report, welcoming its recommendation that the needs of users should drive what official statistics are produced and how.

Three priorities

  1. Restore confidence in labour market statistics.
  2. Give the statistical system the funding to modernise.
  3. Make the independence of statistics clearer in law.

"Good statistics are a public good. This report is a chance to put users first."

RSS spokesperson
