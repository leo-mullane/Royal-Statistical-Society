Data Science Professional

Data Science Professional (DSP) and Advanced Data Science Professional (AdvDSP) are awarded with the Alliance for Data Science Professionals, to a shared standard.

CMSStandard content page: left-hand section menu, rich text with headings, and reusable components (key facts, steps, accordion, call to action) placed by editors. REQ-029, REQ-013, REQ-014

At a glance

Who it is for
Data scientists who meet the Alliance standard, at practitioner (DSP) or advanced (AdvDSP) level
Fee
£[fee] application fee
How long it takes
Usually 8 to 12 weeks
You will need
CV, evidence against the Alliance standard, two referees

How to apply

  1. Join as a Fellow
  2. Apply in MyRSS
  3. Assessment
  4. Award

You apply from MyRSS once you are a member. Your application is assessed by the Professional Affairs Committee, which may ask for more evidence.

Once awarded, you will appear on the public Professional Register unless you choose not to.
