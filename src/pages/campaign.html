The six principles

  1. Transparent

    Say when and how AI was used.

  2. Tested

    Check for bias and error before use.

  3. Accountable

    A named person is responsible.

  4. Quality assured

    The same standards as any other method.

  5. Explained

    Uncertainty is communicated clearly.

  6. Open to challenge

    Users can question the results.
