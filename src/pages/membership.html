What membership gives you

Professional recognition

Use the letters after your name and work towards GradStat, CStat or Data Analyst status.

Journals and Significance

Online access to Series A, B and C, and Significance in print and online.

Training at member rates

Around 25% off public courses, and CPD you can record in MyRSS.

Sections and local groups

Meetings, newsletters and networks in your field and near you.

A voice in policy

Contribute to consultation responses and campaigns.

Directories

Be listed in the Fellows Directory, and in the Consultants Directory if you offer consultancy.

Membership grades

Fellow

The standard grade, for anyone with an interest in statistics or data. No qualifications needed.

From £165 a year

Graduate Statistician (GradStat)

For graduates with a degree that meets our academic standard.

From £185 a year

Chartered Statistician (CStat)

Our highest professional award: qualifications plus at least five years of experience.

From £230 a year

Data Analyst

For people working with data, from apprentices to experienced analysts.

From £165 a year

Fellow or Member of an International Society (FIS/MIS)

For members of partner societies outside the UK.

From £95 a year

e-Student

For full-time students. Digital journals and events at student rates.

From £25 a year
