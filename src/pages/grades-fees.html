Concessions

We offer 25% off for members who are:

  • on a career break or parental leave
  • in their first two years after graduating
  • retired, or aged 60 or over
  • in an eligible developing country (EDA)

Honorary and compounder members pay no annual fee. Students should choose e-Student membership.

Students

e-Student membership costs £25 a year and includes digital journals, events at student rates and the Young Statisticians Section.
