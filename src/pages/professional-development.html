Professional status

Chartered Statistician (CStat)

Statisticians with an approved degree and at least five years of professional experience.

Graduate Statistician (GradStat)

Graduates of an accredited degree, or a degree with enough statistical content.

Data Analyst

Analysts with relevant experience, or who have completed a Level 4 data analyst apprenticeship.

Data Science Professional

Data scientists who meet the Alliance standard, at practitioner (DSP) or advanced (AdvDSP) level.

Continuing professional development (CPD)

Holders of professional status record at least 30 hours of CPD a year. Courses, conferences, reviewing, mentoring and self-directed study all count. RSS courses and events are added to your record automatically.
